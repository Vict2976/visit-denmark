# Design system playbook

This product is an AI chat interface: its components are organized around a conversation thread, individual messages (including branched responses and per-message actions), and a composer for sending prompts with attachments, screenshots, action menus, model-style selects and hover cards. The token set in src/styles.css supplies surface and foreground colors for background, card, popover, overlay and sidebar contexts, a primary family with deep and soft variants, secondary, accent and destructive roles, border/input/ring colors, a five-step chart palette, display and sans typography, a radius scale, card and lift shadows, and hero and red gradients.

## In one screen

- **Theme lives in:** the stylesheet (Tailwind v4, CSS-first, so there is no tailwind.config).
- **Tokens:** 50 in 11 group(s), folded from 87 declaration(s).
- **Themes:** light and dark, switched by `&:is(.dark *)`.
- **Spent:** 49 token(s) are used somewhere; 1 are declared and never used.
- **Components:** 273 declared, 200 primitives, 14 with variants (70 variant value(s)).
- **Disagreements:** 120 instance(s) in 5 kind(s). See the last section.

## Provenance

The primitives are generated by shadcn/ui: `components.json` declares style `new-york`, base colour `slate`, icon library `lucide`, primitives at "@/components/ui", and CSS variables on. A reader who knows that convention knows most of this system before reading any of it.
